Info & Stats


Personality:

Chiaki was born as a very curious child, always wondering around the house finding hidden places, strange items she couldn't understand. This trait made her very open to strangers at first, she tried to please the others by answering their questions however the payback was costly for them. Sometimes her questions could become very pushy and on some occasions they were interpreted as rude always got her scolded by her parents. Her curiosity pushed her towards lots of reading, book were the best place to gain interesting information and views someone else might had.

As she grew her curiosity diminished thanks to the constant study drilling of her teachers they sated the demon inside the child although not completely. Her lessons comprised of many things a child would need to, such as writing, reading, counting, cooking and such. Learning all that normal stuff however became boring in a long run which led to her developing a sneaky side. She would often sneak away in secret to go watch the medics at their hospital try doing their chakra stuff. Chiaki's diligent side could almost grasp the workings of how chakra worked but could never succeed on her own.

The girl grew up to be a very kind and caring person, no doubt the influence from her parents who were herbalists and doctors. They taught her the craft, starting with the basics of being a doctor which is to help no matter what. Chiaki on many attempts to reviving a dead animal didn't want to try giving up, even after they have been diagnosed by a professional, she would stay a day or two longer trying to heal them. After she had given up she would try blaming herself, the girl is pretty hard on herself sometimes. That is a good thing though, her inability pressed her to complete better healing techniques,

From the start she wasn't much of a diplomat, she could be sweet and courteous but there always lacked something. Could it be that she lacked the popular humor many people in power had? Her humour was always somewhat iffy, sarcastic maybe. The fact remained that she didn't like telling jokes too much appearing serious to others. Not many would crack a joke while she was around, they thought she would strike them down if they did.

Chiaki wasn't really a normal teen then later adult, while the other girls were relatively noticing the opposite gender this trend seems to have passed her from one ear to the other side without sinking in. It was always the books that kept her busy instead, the normal school life just passed by her while she read under the tree in a shade. Later when she was admitted to the academy she stopped reading and concentrated more on what was going on in class however the topic of the opposite gender always passed her.
Appearance:




Chiaki has a long red hair that is always let down with a forehead protector on it. Occasionally they would be tied to a pigtail enabling her to move and do work easier. Her face looks pretty delicate which she inherited from her mother, round with soft skin as expected of a teen. She has glowy green eyes shining with wisdom and kindness and a nice wide smile that many of her classmates love. Her small nose complemented the rest of her facial features.

Chiaki's body seems to be mature, developed almost past her teens it would seem although age spoke otherwise. She is quite tall for her age, towering the others by 5 or so cm. Long slender legs added to her curves showed very well under the normal clothes she wore. Her wardrobe changes to suite the situation. When training she wears blue shirt with a ribbon and loose red miniskirt. She also wears a long white and blue coat with a hood but rarely does she put it on. To a feast or special event Chiaki would wear a beautiful red long dress that match with her hair.

After Time Skip:





By this time the girl has turned into a woman, she was no longer growing in the important areas. Her face gained a few imperfections however that was nothing to worry about, any make-up could erase those. Her face was still very clean, pure and had a nice tone to the skin and looked very much younger than her age suggested. She was still tall, but one could just say she was average in that department now, a few others have outgrown her significantly. She gained a bit of a weight too, not a lot to be worth mentioning and more importantly it didn't show.

Her attire has changed a little, these days she fashioned sleeveless shirts of all sorts of color depending on the mood during the day and short trousers usually in blue. After becoming the stand in Hokage she wore a coat which had a Hokage symbol on the back however her term didn't last long and at a later date she stopped wearing the coat, instead it was replaced by a white doctor coat when she retired altogether as a ninja of the village to take up upon her duty to help the weak and sickly.


When Duchess:




Soon after retiring she was called back home to Mo, the origin of her parents where she was given the heirship of the Duchy. By doing many new duties as an heiress her face grew visibly tired, her smile vanished under the pressure of politics but she was still young as ever.

When she became the Duchess of Mo she changed her attire a bit. She wears the dark blue kimono with golden laces and her sash that bounds the the kimono is glossy silver. She wears a white cloak over her left shoulder with Japanese symbols for Duchess. Chiaki's tessen sheath is always oh her back over her right shoulder.

History:

Chiaki was born in the Leaf Village as an Uchiha. Her family lived in a large house that was filled with books. The house was always clean and smelled good due to herbs that were kept in the storage room. Her parents were an ordinary pair of people who's job was to make medicals. Little did Chiaki know, her parents were rebelling against becoming the next in line for Mo. Since she was small, her mom and dad taught her about many uses of herbs. As time passes, Chiaki had a wider and wider knowledge of herbalism that her parents acknowledged. Chiaki's parents intended her to continue in their work so they began to teach her advanced techniques on making medicals from the herbs. Fortunately Chiaki was very interested in the studies and quickly learned what their parents had to teach.

Sometimes in her free time, she would sit under a tree by the meadows near her parent's house and read stories about medical ninjas and their bravery. She reads about how they go and save the lives of their comrades. She liked these stories until becoming a herbalist ninja was her dream. Although she loved and trusted her parents, she never told them about her dream, she thought that they would not understand. At times, Chiaki would sneak away from her parents into the local hospital to watch medical ninjas work with herbs. What she didn't know is that her parents knew about all this. Just before the Uchiha massacre, the whole family got forced back to Mo where the royal guards managed to protect their identifies and thus escaped the whole massacre. Later her parents gave up the right to inherit Mo to their daughter.

When it was time the children could be entered into the academy Chiaki's parents ask her, what she would like to do when she grows up. Chiaki said immediately: "I want to be just like you dad and mum." and she looked away trying to hide her true emotions from the parents. Chiaki's dad smiled and said: "Well we know that is not true. Would you like to become a ninja?. "How did you... know?" Chiaki asked and her heart was beating rapidly as she was getting excited. Chiaki yelled: Yes dad, I want to be a ninja!!"". Her parents looked at each other and both smiled when they told her that she will be enlisted to the academy the next day. By this they were hoping to prepare her for martial prowess that was required of a Duchess.

Soon Chiaki began to go to the ninja academy. She was among the best students in her class, always happy to learn from the teachers. She is known as a hard worker and
a learned, in the ways of herbalism. Chiaki was bright and beautiful, that combination made her very desirable. Her classmates were interested in her which means she also made a lot of enemies on the girls side. At the academy she excelled at Genjutsu. She could outsmart most of the guys in her class and was a model for the others, from the teacher's point of view. Chiaki graduated the academy with top marks and now is pursuing her dream of becoming a ninja.

After some time the teen turned to an adult who would be loved by many in the village leading to her rise as a stand in Hokage. Although her term wasn't a long one she nonetheless gained experiences running a constitution, later resulting in being chosen as an heiress to the Mo Duchy. She couldn't find solace in going into combat and having to take lives and so the only logical decision came to her, retirement. Chiaki became a dedicated herbalist instead helping the weak and sickly with not much money for proper treatment. That also came to a halt right after she was made an heiress to the Mo Duchy and then becoming the Duchess herself. She has reigned for quite some time now, even genetically modified her three daughters.
