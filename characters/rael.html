Appearance [Before Heritage and After Glamour]



With prominent aristocratic facial structures, Rael's graceful face is enhanced by acute, delicate features. His brows are a dark obsidian in color and due to the ease they are up-kept, they can be considered rather immaculate. Prominent bones forming the eye hold deep hooded eyes, being augmented by Rael's piercing, hypnotic amber colored irises. Cheeks are sallow and gaunt, nose to a point, with a sharp contour, complementing pale, thin lips that are often formed in a manic, Cheshire grin. Silk-like hair, onyx black in color, waves slightly towards his not-so-broad shoulders. The hair parts slightly in the middle, allowing the black waves to be tucked behind elegant, pointed elf-like ears, and revealing a proportionate forehead. Strands of hair wisp here and there, falling into place, acclimating his gaunt and keen ovaline feature of his face.

Pale, waxen, almost as if his skin was bloodless, describes the fine complexion that adorns the regal visage of Rael. Along with this regal visage, is the elegant body that maintains and delivers the grace and beauty his body provides. Arms are lithe, flexible, with barely, but noticeable, amount of muscle. These fine limbs are accented with his cold, ashen coloured hands, adorned with long, elegant fingers. His physique is fragile in appearances and actuality. Like his arms, only slight definition of muscles could be seen. His abdomen and chest is hairless and smooth, comparable to a waxen mannequin. His height, accented by his lean and flexible legs, raises to approximately five feet and nine inches.

Rael's usual attire is rather grim and monochromatic black. Consisting of a velvet-laced ivory black shirt, buttoning up to where only his collar bones protrude out of his ashen complexioned skin, showing off how small he truly is when it comes to the physical side. The black shirt is adorned with intricate Gothic designs his mother used to love. Rael's pants follow in a similar fashion. Smooth, black velvet material with the same intricate Gothic designs formatting his pants, fitted his lean, structured lithe legs. As an outer coat, covering his torso down to his ankles, a matte, smooth, and durable black trench coat with no design cascades down his frame. To compliment his wardrobe, black dress shoes embellish and ornate his distinguished features.

Appearance after heritage and before glamour



After he awakened his bloodline, Rael's form was drastically changed. His father was an Archdevil, glamouring as a simple mortal, thus making Rael half-devil. Through the awakening, his eyes, instead of the deep amber color, turn into a deep, blood orange in color. Two horns have also grown into his skull, slightly protruding from the base of his hairline. The total length of both of them are about eight inches in length, both of them slightly curving forwards with a sharp point. At will, they will glow a vibrant silver in color, hypnotizing to all that sees them. His hair, from the darkening black, gains a glow-like color of a dull red. His skin, pale, sickly white, is now replaced with a dull angry red complexion.

Other noticeable, physical changes, is a prehensile tail, about three feet in length, with the thickness being about 6 inches in diameter starting from the base of his spine, slowly getting smaller as it reaches to a sharp point at the end. His fingers, more sharp and boney, hold slightly larger, and dangerous claws that are quite durable in strength. Rael's Height had stayed the same in the conversion.

Personality
All men want is life is to have power, control over someone else. With Rael's liking and taking to the illusion-based arts, striking fear into a victim's heart, seeing them tremble and quake beneath him, gives a power trip and pleasure to his being. Seeing the life slowly escape through his victims eyes, but only to stop and see it return, pumps adrenaline through his body, giving him an euphoric high. Addicted to this feeling, he actively seeks to induce nightmares onto innocents and watch them suffer through his 'game'. But enabling fear into others does not exclude himself from his own fear. Despite the euphoria he gets, and the constant yearning for such an ct, he has enough control when needed. Rael will not engage in such act when around others who are stronger and could easily decimate him.
Through the his own search of ancient and rather perverse knowledge, he has come across many of great things and has seen these happenings. From seeing people tremble at their own fears, bloody dismemberment, deep gashes that turn life threatening, and gaining his vice from it, most people do not see that he has fears. But the inevitable part of life, death, is something Rael fears the most. Being taken from a life where he wants to create havoc, play with people and see them suffer by his own hands, Rael searches for ways to elude death and subside his own fears of it.
Rael may not be a tactician genius or intelligent being, but years messing around with his art has enabled his creative mind to be honed. Seeing minute details of people, his environment, and body language he observes, he is able to gradually control a situation to forth come to his benefit. Rael knows his limitations and will not confront something or someone that he knows he will not be able to defeat, a straight fight or espionage. From his own experience, rushing through things and plans never got him far. Rael has learned that waiting from the shadows, listening to the inept rumors and pointless gossip, observing his target with a scrutinizing gaze, gives him the knowledge to tear down the victim's barriers for his illusions to have better performance. With better performance gives his illustrious illusionary art the umph it needs.

Given his sharp, delicate features, Rael would consider himself a pretty boy with a modest allure, bewitching the unexpected minds of others. Without any shame in his core, he will flirt, woe, and seduce any bystanders if it meant his survival or in order to reach his own goal. Always having a devious grin, which most find unsettling and untrustworthy, shows that his mind is always circulating with nasty ideas. May it be male or female, preferably male, he will use his wit and pretty mouth to flirt, weave misgivings and gain favor or trust. With this sense of self preservation, he has tried to come out of a fight unscathed and without fighting, often met with threats of his demise, or how much of a coward he is.

With his own vice of sadism, it spreads out more into his personality. He enjoys crude, and dark humor getting laughs and chuckles from the everyday misgivings people have to suffer through. May it consist of pain, trauma, death, or what ever, as long as it doesn't effect his being, will edge on and make the matter worse for his target of interest. When he ventures into villages, he loves to prey on susceptible humans, always having a devious illusion on his tongue. One of his greatest traits that has left him alive to this very day, is his innate ability of self preservation. Once a situation arises in which he might be in trouble or seriously harmed, Rael will try his damned hardest to wiggle out of the situation with the least amount of sacrifices to his being.

Background

Come forth from the shadows...
On a moonless Hallows eve, screams of labor induced pain echoed the house. The midwife tried to soothe the lady with small gestures, but the woman wasn't having any of it. She was screaming profanities to the husband outside the room, threatening to rip of his genitals if he were to get near her with it ever again. Midnight passed, the child yet to come forth from the womb. A few hours later, the witching hour came to be, and with a struggled yell, the mother finally pushed the damned child out. Crying could be heard as the midwife snipped the cord and wrapped the black haired baby and handed it to the mother. "It's a boy..." The mother smiled softly, kissing the child's forehead. "Welcome to the world, Rael." The mother said with a content sigh. Before she could pass out and drop her son, the midwife grabbed Rael and called forth the husband to see his newly found son.

Gargled and high pitched sequels could be heard by anyone traveling down the bazaar. Many people turned heads in distaste, but usually, one look at the baby making the noise, their attitude for some shape or reason, always changed. Like an allure bewitched their minds and saw reason to like the child. The parents knew that this wasn't normal, but counted it as a blessing and didn't hate the child for it.

Rael was always a curious child and found a way into his parents study and found many books that caught his interest. Books on chakra, even though it was basic, devoured a good portion of his time, even though he couldn't mold chakra yet. Other books he read were on minor rituals involving the dead and augmentation of the human body. He would keep these books in mind for when he gets older.
From death comes the artist...
After constant bugging his parents, Rael was finally able to go outside the village with them to a local merchant community. His father was a local librarian, and he always look out for rare, or valuable books, while his mother was the merchant and held connections to help further both their goals. Wealth and knowledge. It was a bright day, sun high in the sky warming up the atmosphere, but to their lack of knowledge, and ambush waited for the three. In hindsight, Rael should have questioned what his parents dealt with. Rael's eyes glazed over and he took hold of the knife his father gave him for protection. He knew he was being controlled, but he couldn't fight it. Something knocked his parents on the ground, but without hesitation, Rael was upon both of them, with the knife high in the air, ready to strike. One emotion crossed the parent's eyes, fear, and Rael saw that. Despite being his parents and being controlled, he willingly smirked, the feeling he felt, the fear he saw, it excited him. Without thinking and without control, the knife dived down twice, striking their hearts, killing them instantly. Soon after the woman who put said control on them walked up to him. She grabbed the knife and stabbed him in a non fatal place on the body, if he didn't bleed out before help arrived. But he saw her perform handseals and saw a whispy being exit from his parents. He thought it was their soul being extracted, but he soon after passed out.

Starting forth, Master of Fear...
Being rescued by the nearest hidden village, which was Konohagakure, he established a goal to become a ninja. He discovered that his mother was related to the Nara clan that resided in the village. The abilities that the Clan possessed fascinated him and sought to be taught by the shadow weavers. He researched what that woman did to his parents and found that some people are able to manipulate souls, called soulcasters. Eventually he would gain that ability and put a stopper to his death. He continued to practice the illusion arts along side the Nara's abilities and eventually, become a Genin.
